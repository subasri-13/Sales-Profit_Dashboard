PROJECT EXPLANATION

This Sales & Profit Dashboard project analyzes business sales data to understand performance trends, profitability, and customer distribution across different dimensions. The dashboard provides clear insights into sales growth, profit contribution, and business performance across regions, states, and product categories.

It helps users understand:

Total sales and profit performance

Customer and order distribution

Sales performance by region and state

Profit contribution by shipping modes

Segment-wise business performance

Top performing product sub-categories

Sales Dashboard Insights

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays important business metrics:

Total Customers: 3,394

Total Orders: 3,394

Average Sales: 1.01K

Total Sales: 3.43M
Overall Sales Contribution: 798.11K
These KPIs provide a quick overview of the company's overall sales performance.


Sales by State

A horizontal bar chart displays total sales by state.
Key observations:
California generates the highest sales revenue.
Other strong-performing states include New York and Texas.
Helps identify high-performing geographic markets.

Sales by Ship Mode

The dashboard compares sales across different shipping methods.
Standard Class contributes the highest sales.
Followed by Second Class and First Class shipping.
Same Day delivery contributes the least.
This helps understand logistics preferences and customer shipping choices.

Sales by Segment

Sales are analyzed across three major customer segments:
Consumer Segment – highest contribution
Corporate Segment – second highest
Home Office Segment – lowest contribution
This shows that the business primarily targets individual consumers

Sales by Region

Regional sales distribution shows:
West Region – highest sales (~1.12M)
East Region – second highest (~1.01M)
Central Region – moderate sales
South Region – lowest sales
This insight helps businesses focus on high-performing regions.

Top Sales by Ship Mode

A pie chart visualizes the percentage contribution of each shipping method to total sales.
Key insights:
Standard Class shipping dominates sales contribution.
Other shipping modes contribute smaller portions
